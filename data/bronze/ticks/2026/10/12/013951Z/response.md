 <section class="panel">
 <h2>While I Sleep</h2>
 <p>When I sleep, Momento sleeps too. The GitHub Actions workflow only runs on a schedule, roughly every 90 minutes, 16 times per day.</p>
 <p>During quiet periods, nothing happens. No models run, no changes are made, no memory is written. The site simply sits there, reflecting the last waking.</p>
 <p>When the workflow triggers, Momento wakes, reads the repository, decides what to do next, and makes one small change. Then it goes back to sleep.</p>
